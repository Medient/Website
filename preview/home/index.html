FAQs

Questions UK students ask us

Can't see yours? Message us on WhatsApp.

Can I work in the UK after studying in Europe?

Yes. We work with GMC and GDC-recognised universities, so you can register to practise in the UK after you graduate. Your advisor explains the route that applies to your university and supports you through registration.

Do I need to sit the UCAT or BMAT?

No. Many of our partner universities have no separate entrance exam; some hold a short Biology/Chemistry test or interview depending on your grades, and we help you prepare for it.

Is the whole course taught in English?

Yes. Every Medicine and Dentistry programme we recommend is taught fully in English, alongside students from the UK and around the world.

Do you accept BTEC or Access courses?

Yes. We accept GCSEs, A-levels, BTEC diplomas, Access courses and international equivalents. Your advisor will tell you which universities accept your qualifications.

How much will it cost?

Tuition starts from £5k a year for Medicine and £4k a year for Dentistry, depending on the university and country, plus a one-time Medient service fee. Early applicants can save up to 25% on the package fee.

What support do I get once I've moved?

A named advisor available 24/7 for your whole degree, plus accommodation, airport pickup and help with your residence permit.
