Studying Dentistry in Europe: what to expect, and whether you qualify

Everything you need to know before your consultation: how the degree works, what you'll study and what universities ask for.

Duration5–6 years
DegreeDoctor of Dental Medicine (DMD)
Taught in100% English
IntakesSeptember / October*
Tuition fees£4k+ per year

What you'll study

  1. Years 1–2Biomedical sciences

    Anatomy, physiology, biochemistry and microbiology, with an introduction to dental materials and oral biology.

  2. Years 2–3Pre-clinical simulation

    Hands-on training on phantom heads in simulation labs: restorative dentistry, prosthodontics and endodontics.

  3. Years 3–5Supervised patient care

    Treating real patients under supervision: conservative dentistry, periodontology, orthodontics, oral surgery and paediatric dentistry.

  4. Final yearClinical practice

    Comprehensive patient care in university dental clinics that prepares you for graduation and registration as a dentist.

After graduating: you'll hold a degree from a GDC-recognised university, so you can register with the GDC to practise in the UK, or practise across the EU.

Entry requirements

Qualifications
A-levels, BTEC, Access courses, IB or international equivalents. We review your GCSEs too. Grade requirements vary by university and are far more flexible than UK offers.
Prerequisite subjects
Biology and Chemistry (A-level or equivalent) are preferred. Some universities accept one science alongside Physics or Maths.
Admissions tests
No UCAT or BMAT. Many universities have no entrance exam; some hold a short Biology/Chemistry test or interview depending on your grades, and we help you prepare.
English
No IELTS or TOEFL needed for UK applicants.
Documents to start
A scan of your passport ID page, plus GCSE / A-level transcripts showing all subjects and marks. Transfer students also send current university transcripts.
Budget
A tuition budget of at least £4,000 a year, plus living costs (around £400–£700 a month in Europe).
Check if I qualify

You're likely a strong fit if…

  • You missed a UK offer, or don't want to rely on UCAT or Clearing
  • You've studied Biology and Chemistry (or are studying them now)
  • You or your family can fund at least £4,000 a year in tuition, plus living costs
  • You're ready to live abroad for 5–6 years with our team behind you

It may not be right if…

  • You need UK Student Finance to cover your tuition
  • Your budget is under £4,000 a year
  • You'd need a loan to pay your tuition
  • You're not prepared to study a full degree outside the UK

*Intakes and fees vary by university and course. Your advisor confirms exact details on your free consultation.

FAQs

Questions UK students ask us

Can I work as a dentist in the UK after studying in Europe?

Yes. We work with GDC-recognised universities, so you can register with the GDC and practise in the UK after you graduate. We support you through registration, so you have a clear path home if that's your goal.

Do I need to sit the UCAT or an entrance exam?

You don't need the UCAT or BMAT. Many of our partner universities have no separate entrance exam; some hold a short Biology/Chemistry test or interview depending on your grades, and we help you prepare for it.

Do I need IELTS or another English test?

Not if you're applying from the UK. Universities may only ask for proof of English from students who didn't study in English at school.

Do you accept BTEC or Access courses?

Yes. We accept GCSEs, A-levels, BTEC diplomas, Access courses and international equivalents. Your advisor will tell you which universities accept your qualifications.

Is the whole course taught in English?

Yes. Every programme we recommend is taught fully in English, alongside students from the UK and around the world.

What if my A-level grades aren't what I hoped?

Entry requirements are far more flexible than in the UK. Book a free consultation and your advisor will tell you honestly which universities you qualify for.

How much will it cost in total?

Tuition fees are £4k+ per year depending on the university and country, plus a one-time Medient service fee. Early applicants can save up to 25% on the package fee.

What support do I get once I've moved?

A named advisor available 24/7 for your whole degree, plus accommodation, airport pickup and help legalising your stay, all at no extra charge.
